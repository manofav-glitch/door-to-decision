## 0. Ground rules taken from the note

- **The organ, not the number**: severe BP without acute organ damage is not an emergency; no IV drugs.
- **Three tempos**: minutes (dissection, SCAPE, severe pre-eclampsia, ICH, phaeochromocytoma);
  hours (MAP down 20–25%: malignant hypertension, encephalopathy, AKI); permissive (ischaemic stroke).
- **Prescribe a target with a time and a floor** ("MAP 105–115 by 60 min, not below 100").
- **Never**: sublingual nifedipine, oral clonidine loading, reflex furosemide in malignant hypertension.
- **India layer**: intermittent IV labetalol boluses as the pragmatic default; oral nifedipine in pregnancy
  where pumps are scarce; Pritchard magnesium; scorpion envenomation; glomerulonephritis; give the first
  doses before referral.
