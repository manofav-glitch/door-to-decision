# Case 3 (cp-03) — ChatGPT image prompts

Made by the content owner in ChatGPT. 8 pictures, made as TWO sheets of four square panels
(a 2×2 grid per image gives ~620 px panels, twice the size of a 4×2 sheet). Send both sheets to
Claude, who cuts them into `content/assets/art/cp-03/01…08`.

How to use
1. New ChatGPT chat. Upload ONE earlier picture that shows only the doctor and the nurse (case 2
   panel 5, the two of them at the computer). Don't upload one with an earlier patient in it.
2. Paste **Setup**, then **New characters**, then **Sheet 1**, then **Sheet 2**.
3. If a face or the style drifts, say "Match the reference image exactly" and regenerate.

Morning light throughout (the case starts at 8:40 a.m.). Panel 6 of the case (a monitor close-up)
stays code-drawn, so it isn't here.

```

- Style: exactly like the reference image I've uploaded: muted retro storybook illustration, flat gouache colours, soft shading, visible paper grain, clean dark ink outlines. Warm, slightly desaturated palette: cream, teal, dusty orange, slate blue, olive.
- Setting: a busy government-hospital emergency department in India, in the MORNING (soft early daylight). Realistic and respectful.
- The ED DOCTOR (young man, curly black hair, glasses, white coat over navy scrubs) and the NURSE (woman, low bun, teal scrubs) are the same people as in the reference image.
- NO text of any kind: no letters, numbers, signs, labels, logos, watermarks or speech bubbles. Screens and papers show only faint lines.
Reply "ready" and wait for my prompts.
```
