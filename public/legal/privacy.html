Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Door to Decision is run by Manav, in India (“we”). This page explains what we collect and why. We collect as little as we can.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile picture and a Google account ID, through Google’s Firebase Authentication.
  • When you pay: the amount, the date, and Razorpay’s reference numbers for the payment, linked to your account, plus the date your access ends.

We never receive your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay handles those under its own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

Your progress, scores, Revise deck, settings and offline downloads are stored in your browser on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser’s data for this site deletes them.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your data, except with the services below that run the app.

Services that run the app

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, and storing who has paid and until when.
  • Razorpay: taking payments.
  • Cloudflare: hosting the app and confirming payments.

Their servers may be outside India.

Why we use your data

To let you sign in, to know which units you have unlocked and until when, and to answer support and refund requests.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and payment records while you use the app, and payment records for as long as Indian accounting and tax law requires.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be deleted, by emailing us. Deleting your account ends any paid access. Payment records the law requires us to keep are kept until that period ends.

Age

The app is meant for doctors and medical students, and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new date.
