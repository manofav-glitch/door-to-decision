Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Cancellation

Access is a one-time payment of ₹151 for 365 days. It is not a subscription and never renews automatically, so there is nothing to cancel.

Refunds

The paid units unlock as soon as your payment is confirmed, so we do not give refunds once access has been unlocked.

We always refund in full:

  • Failed payments: money left your account but the payment failed and the units did not unlock. Banks and Razorpay usually return these automatically.
  • Duplicate payments: you were charged twice for the same access.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of our confirming them. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Paid but still locked?

Usually the units unlock within seconds. If they are still locked an hour after paying, email us and we will either unlock them or refund you.

How to ask

Email manav.upums@gmail.com with the email address of your Google account and the payment ID from Razorpay’s receipt (it starts with pay_).
